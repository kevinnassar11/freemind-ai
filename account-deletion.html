FreeMind Privacy Policy

Delete your account

You can permanently remove your FreeMind account and everything tied to it at any time. If the app is installed you don't need to contact anyone.

Open Settings

Sign in to FreeMind, then open the Settings tab.

Find Account

Scroll to the Account section.

Tap Delete account

It is written in red, and you are asked to confirm once before anything happens.

Confirm

Deletion starts immediately and can't be reversed.

This can't be undone. There is no recovery period and no way to restore a deleted account.

No app installed?

Email kevinnassar80@gmail.com with the subject Account Deletion Request, from the address you signed in with. We'll delete your data within 30 days and confirm by email.
