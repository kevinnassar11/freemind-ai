# Account Deletion — FreeMind AI

You can permanently delete your FreeMind AI account and all associated data at any time.

## Option 1: In the app (recommended, immediate)

1. Open FreeMind AI and sign in.
2. Go to **Settings**.
3. Under **Account**, tap **Delete account**.
4. Confirm the deletion.

This immediately and permanently removes:
- Your profile (email, display name, photo, theme preference)
- Your entire place search history
- All of your favorite places

**This cannot be undone.**

## Option 2: Request deletion without the app

If you no longer have the app installed, email **[your support email here]** from the email address associated with your account, with the subject line "Account Deletion Request." Include:
- The email address used to sign in to FreeMind AI

We will process your request and permanently delete your account data within 30 days, and confirm by email once complete.

## What Gets Deleted

| Data | Deleted? |
|---|---|
| Profile (email, name, photo, theme) | Yes |
| Place search history | Yes |
| Favorite places | Yes |
| Push notification token | Yes |

No data is retained after deletion. See our [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md) for more detail on what we collect and why.
