FreeMind AI ← Privacy Policy

Delete your account

You can permanently remove your FreeMind AI account and everything tied to it at any time — no need to contact anyone if the app is installed.

Open Settings

Sign in to FreeMind AI, then go to the Settings tab.

Find Account

Scroll to the Account section.

Tap Delete account

Written in red — you'll be asked to confirm once before anything happens.

Confirm

Deletion is immediate and permanent from this point.

This can't be undone. There's no recovery window and no way to restore a deleted account.

No app installed?

Email support@freemind.ai with subject line Account Deletion Request, from the address you signed in with. We'll delete your data within 30 days and confirm by email.
