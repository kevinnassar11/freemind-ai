Privacy Policy

Last updated Sep 14, 2026 Applies to the FreeMind AI Android app

FreeMind AI helps you search places, save favorites, and keep a private history of your searches. This policy explains exactly what we collect, why, and how to remove it.

What we collect

Two kinds of data: what your Google account hands us at sign-in, and what the app itself generates as you use it.

From sign-in

  • Email address
  • Display name
  • Profile photo URL

From using the app

  • Searches — query text, mode, and top result place_search_history
  • Saved places — name, address, coordinates favorite_places
  • Theme preference & notification token
  • No precise real-time location — search is text-based, not GPS-driven
  • No payment information — the app has no purchases
  • Nothing purchased or collected from third-party data brokers

How it's used

Strictly to run the app for you: showing your own history and favorites back to you, keeping your session signed in, and — only if you've turned them on — sending update notifications. We do not sell data, and we do not share it for advertising.

Where it's stored

App data lives in a Supabase-hosted PostgreSQL database with row-level security enabled on every table — each query is scoped to your own authenticated user ID by policy, at the database layer, not just in the app's code. Sign-in itself is handled by Firebase Authentication.

Third-party services

Firebase AuthenticationGoogle sign-in
Google Maps SDK & Places APIMap rendering & place search
SupabaseSearch history, favorites, profile storage

Each provider processes data under its own privacy policy for the portion that runs on its platform.

Retention & deletion

You can permanently delete your account and everything tied to it at any time, from Settings → Delete account in the app. This can't be undone. Full instructions, including a route that doesn't require the app, are on the account deletion page.

Children's privacy

FreeMind AI isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 13.

If this policy changes materially, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
